How to read it

The chapters follow CRISP-DM, the Cross-Industry Standard Process for Data Mining, because the product was built that way: understand the problem, understand the data, prepare it, model it, evaluate the models honestly, then deploy and keep watching. Chapters 7 to 9 step outside that cycle for process models, the user guide and standards.

Every chapter opens with In short, a plain-language summary anyone can read. Boxes marked with an audience give a little extra for that reader. Badges at the top of each chapter say who it is mainly for.

Students

Read 8.1 Finding a printer that works. Two minutes.

Student staff

The summary, then the user guide, especially Overview, Rounds and Investigations.

Supervisors

Business understanding, the user guide, and process models.

IT leadership

The summary, evaluation and deployment, then the standards.

Engineers

Data preparation, the data dictionary, architecture and the appendices.

Data scientists

Data understanding through evaluation; the model inventory is in chapter 4.
