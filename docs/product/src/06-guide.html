Using ResNet Print Ops

StudentsStudent staffSupervisorsIT leadership
In short

Sign in with the account your ResNet administrator gave you. The left side lists every page; the top bar holds the filter, the time period, export, the activity log, notifications, appearance and the Assistant. Everything is read-only except investigations, your own account and feature requests.

8.1 For students: finding a printer that works

  1. Scan the QR sign on the printer, where your residence hall or building has one.
  2. The page shows whether that printer is working right now and, if not, the nearest printers that are, with the walking time. East Campus Commons counts as open to everyone; other residence-hall printers need card access.
  3. Wepa holds print jobs online, so a job you sent can usually be released at another working station.

Student status pages are behind sign-in unless the administrator turns on public status pages (with Wepa's and BSU's agreement). They show the present only, never history.

8.2 Getting started (staff)

  1. Open the app's address and sign in. A temporary password from the administrator must be changed the first time.
  2. Use a password of 12 or more characters; a few unrelated words works well. Common and breached passwords are refused.
  3. Under your picture: My account (picture, phone, password), Directory, Suggest a feature, Sign out.
  4. Choose an appearance (BSU, light, dark or automatic; larger text; compact; reduced motion; extra contrast).

8.4 Common tasks

Respond to an outage

  1. On Overview, open the top item in the work queue, or go to Rounds for a route.
  2. Fix it on site. The station turns Operational within a minute of Wepa's page updating.
  3. The outage resolves itself with its reference number, duration and desk-hour split. Nothing to type.

Work an investigation

  1. Open Investigations. Detector-opened cases are marked; you can also open one yourself.
  2. Assign it (moves to Analyze), record a root cause (Respond), record the action taken (Review).
  3. A second person closes it. Download the evidence packet (PDF) for a Wepa case.

Report the month

  1. Open Executive, pick the month, read the highlights.
  2. Use Export in the top bar for PDF, Excel, CSV or JSON of the current filter and period.

Suggest a feature or report a problem

  1. Under your picture, choose Suggest a feature.
  2. Pick the kind, give a title and details, and send. It gets a REQ number and becomes a GitHub issue; your name is not included unless the administrator turned that on.

8.6 Frequently asked questions

Why does a station say "No signal"?
It hasn't appeared on Wepa's page for 5 minutes. Its status is unknown, and that time is left out of every rate.
Why "support unavailable until …"?
The team that supports that printer has no desk open right now; the time is when it next opens.
Why does a figure say "insufficient data"?
Too few incidents or too little observed time to be reliable. It appears once there is enough evidence.
Why isn't the outage-risk model shown?
It hasn't beaten the simple baseline on held-out weeks yet; the page says which condition is not met.
Is "In progress" ever shown?
No. The monitor can see that a problem is open, not whether someone is working on it, so it says Ongoing.
