Chapter 9 Standards, ethics and recommendations

Standards and recommendations

IT leadershipEngineersData scientists
In short

The product was built with recognized standards and professional codes in mind. This chapter maps each one to what the product does, says honestly where it falls short, and recommends next steps. It is a self-assessment: no certification or formal audit is claimed.

9.3 ASA Ethical Guidelines for Statistical Practice

Guideline areaIn practice
Professional integrity and accountabilityMethods named and documented; sample sizes on every statistic; uncertainty shown as intervals
Integrity of data and methodsRaw data never edited; censoring handled (Kaplan–Meier); confounding controlled (Mantel–Haenszel); models gated against a baseline
Responsibilities to stakeholdersPlain-language summaries; "not enough evidence" said plainly; correlation not presented as cause
Responsibilities to data subjectsMinimal personal data; staff directory visibility limited by role

A.4 Glossary

Availability
Share of observed time a printer could print.
Censored
An incident whose start or end is outside what was observed; kept, but handled so it doesn't bias averages.
CRISP-DM
Cross-Industry Standard Process for Data Mining: business understanding, data understanding, data preparation, modeling, evaluation, deployment.
CTQ
Critical to quality: a measurable characteristic that matters to the customer.
CVE, GHSA
Public identifiers for a security vulnerability (Common Vulnerabilities and Exposures; GitHub Security Advisory).
Degraded
Printing, with a warning.
Desk hours
When a support team's desk is staffed.
EPSS
Exploit Prediction Scoring System: probability a CVE is exploited in the next 30 days.
KEV
CISA's Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog.
MTBF, MTTR
Mean time between failures; mean time to repair.
No signal
No reading for 5 minutes; status unknown.
Observed time
Time covered by readings, at most 5 minutes per reading.
Out of service
Cannot print (a red row on Wepa's page).
ResNet
BSU's residential network and technology support team, which supports the residence-hall printers.
RSR
ResNet Support Representative: a student staff member.
SIPOC
Suppliers, inputs, process, outputs, customers.
